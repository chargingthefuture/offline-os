Phone script

Lead with what you want. Ask the questions and let them describe the job.

  1. Open

    "Hi, my name is Farah. I have a Class A CDL and I'm looking for a full-time spotter position at a high-volume yard, where the shift is trailer moves only. I want as much seat time as I can get."

  2. Zero experience, said plainly

    "I want to be upfront: I have zero backing experience. I need a position that trains from scratch and gives new people one to two weeks to get up to speed. Do you have spotter openings that accept that?"

    If they hesitate or say "some experience preferred," jump to step 6.

  3. Ask about the job

    • "What does a typical shift look like?"
    • "About how many trailer moves per shift?"
    • "Who trains me, and for how long?"
    • "Has this site taken a brand-new spotter from you before, and did that person work out?"
    • "Is there a second shift or night shift?"

    Listen for the red words below. Tap them as you hear them. Nights and weekends are easier to get and quieter to learn on.

  4. Start date

    • "I'm available to start immediately. How soon could I start?"
    • "What has to happen before my first shift, like a drug test, background check, or road test? How long does each one take?"
    • "Is the drug test urine, mouth swab, or hair?"
    • "Can I come in today or tomorrow to do the paperwork and testing?"

    Bring your documents to the first visit so paperwork can be done the same day. If the start is two weeks or more away, keep calling other agencies.

  5. Pay, hours, location

    • "Is pay weekly? Do you offer a pay card or daily pay?"
    • "When would my first paycheck come?"
    • "I want as many hours as I can get. Is overtime or extra shifts available, even half days on the weekend?"
    • "What's the exact address of the site, and what time does the shift start and end?"
    • "Do I need steel-toe boots or a safety vest, and do you provide them?"
    • "Is there a locker or safe place on site for my bag?"

    Put the address in your map app on walking directions before you say yes. Two hours one way is your limit. Many agencies pay one week behind, so the first check can take up to two weeks.

  6. Backing only

    "I'm only looking for spotter-only positions. Does this job include any loading or unloading, dock work, forklift or pallet jack, shuttle runs or street moves, yard checks, gate or clerk duties, or trailer cleanouts?"

  7. Get it in writing

    "Great. Can you text or email me confirming that zero backing experience is okay and that the job is trailer moves only?"

    If they won't put it in a text or email, treat it as a no.

  8. If it's not a fit

    "I understand. Please keep my number if a spotter-only job comes up that trains from zero. Thank you."

On arrival

  1. Before you clock in

    "Before I clock in, I want to confirm two things: this position accepts zero backing experience with one to two weeks to learn, and the work is trailer moves only, with no loading, dock work, forklift, shuttle runs, yard checks, or gate duties. Is that right?"

    If the answer is anything but yes, walk.

Have ready

  • CDL and DOT medical card
  • Social Security card, plus birth certificate or passport
  • The agency's text or email confirming zero experience and trailer moves only
