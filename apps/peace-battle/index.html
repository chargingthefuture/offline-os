Where the numbers come from

the college trained in Greek and Latin and mathematics, 2,000 men; and these men trained full 50,000 others in morals and manners, and they in turn taught thrift and the alphabet to nine millions of men … It was a miracle—the most wonderful peace-battle of the 19th century

W. E. B. Du Bois, The Talented Tenth, in The Negro Problem (1903). Two thousand to fifty thousand is twenty-five times over; fifty thousand to nine million is a hundred and eighty times over. Those two rates are the only numbers this game runs on.

They are Du Bois’s figures, written in 1903 to make an argument, not an audit. The full book is committed alongside this game at sources/peace-battle/ so they can be checked, and is public domain at gutenberg.org/ebooks/15041.
