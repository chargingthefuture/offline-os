What this is

Where the board came from

147 people, because that is how many are on the Charging The Future Directory. Not those people: the shape of them. How many hold one skill and how many hold nine, which parts of the work they sit in, how thin each capability is, and roughly where they are. Everybody here was generated from those counts, and no name, place or skill was read from anybody's listing.

The map is at country grain for the same reason. Fifty-four cities hold seventy people between them, so a pin on a city would mostly be one person with one distinctive trade, which is that person to anybody who has seen the list.

Thirteen jobs, and a place runs or it does not

Every place needs all thirteen filled by somebody who can be found: water, power, food, care, teaching, making and mending, and the rest. You cannot cover a missing one with more of something else. A place with nobody in a job has stopped running, however many people are standing in it.

A job held by one person is the fragile kind. The year that person is out of reach, the job is empty and the place stops. That is why finding more people matters as much as adding more skills.

How a year goes

Three actions, then the year resolves on its own. You never tell a person what to do. You decide what exists for people to find: where the Directory reaches, what gets taught, who gets looked for, and whether the arithmetic gets answered.

The dice choose which and when, never whether. Which person is reached this year, which skill turns up, which place isolation grows in. A good run is not a lucky one.

Why teaching is the only thing that finishes it

Teaching reaches people in proportion to how many already do that work. One person teaches one; sixty-eight teach the board. Du Bois counted it: 2,000 trained 50,000, who taught nine millions.

So the thin parts of the list are the hard part. Three of the twenty carry a dozen or more skills and rest on one person each, and nobody with those skills walks in by chance, because that is how rare they are in the pool. Only teaching reaches them.

The two figures

Settled is what was actually exchanged. Waiting is what the open posts would add if every one of them closed, and most never do. Both are a relative index in the spirit of GDP. Neither is money, a price, or anything anybody can cash.

Winning and losing

Win by having all 657 skills present in the same year. 657 is what the list held on 2026-09-13; it grows, so the number here is the one this was built with. Lose when two generations pass and it is not done. There is no opponent: what you are playing against is the clock and isolation.
