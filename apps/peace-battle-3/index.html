How this works

Four people, each with one skill, on a map of places that need something. Tap a person, then tap a place next to them to move, or Help here to work where they stand. Four actions, then the round ends.

What spreads on this board is isolation — a place where somebody needs something and nobody findable can do it. Each round it grows in two more places. A place already at three cuts off, and pushes its isolation into every place beside it. Six cut off and it is over.

Helping in a place whose need matches your skill clears twice as much, and clearing a place to nothing puts it in the Directory: it is covered from then on, its people count, and isolation never returns to it. Cover the whole map and every person on it is reachable.

Nobody wins this alone and nobody wins it against anybody. That is the difference between this and Peace-Battle 2, where one board carries Lizzie Magie’s 1932 rules and somebody has to end up with everything.

The places are real cities and the figures are their rounded populations. Nothing else here is a claim about them.
